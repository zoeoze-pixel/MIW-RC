Illustration cross-check: The attached Supplementary Illustration uses S$6,000 annually for five years and a 4.25% assumed crediting rate. At those inputs, this calculator is calibrated to closely reproduce the illustrated policy values, including approximately S$32,941 at Year 5, S$38,438 at Year 10 and S$58,280 at Year 20. Surrender values are not modeled.

Model assumptions: Premium is added at the start of each applicable policy year. The displayed annual charge applies for Policy Years 1–10. Positive assumed index returns are capped at 9% p.a.; negative assumed returns receive a 10% downside buffer. The attached illustration states that deductions occur monthly and interest is credited on the Segment Maturity Date; this calculator therefore remains an annual approximation. It is not an insurer-issued policy illustration. Confirm actual policy values and terms using official Manulife documents.
